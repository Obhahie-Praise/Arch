# Arch — Product Definition

## What Is Arch?

Arch is an opportunity intelligence and application platform.

It helps users discover opportunities, understand their relevance, and pursue them.

## Initial Opportunity Types

V1 focuses on:

- Jobs
- Grants
- Hackathons

Other opportunity types can be added later.

## Target User

Arch is designed for people actively looking for opportunities that can advance their work, career, projects, education, or goals.

This includes users such as:

- developers
- designers
- founders
- students
- researchers
- professionals
- creatives

The product should not require users to fit one professional identity.

## What Arch Provides

Arch provides:

- opportunity discovery
- structured opportunity information
- personalized matching
- eligibility and requirement analysis
- opportunity saving
- pursuit tracking
- application preparation
- application status tracking

## What Arch Does Not Do

V1 does not aim to:

- autonomously submit applications
- replace application portals
- become a generic AI chatbot
- provide a large analytics dashboard
- support every opportunity type immediately

## Core Principle

Arch should not simply answer:

> "What opportunities exist?"

It should help answer:

> "Which opportunities are worth my attention, why, and what should I do next?"
